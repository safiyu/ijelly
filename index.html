Six looks for your Jellyfin library

Paste one line of CSS into the Jellyfin dashboard and your library, detail pages and video player take on a new design. Built for Jellyfin 12, on desktop and on TV.

Apple TV Cinematheque Noir Editorial Gallery Blueprint Poster-Tinted

Apple TV

Dark glass bars, pill navigation and rounded cards, with a floating two-row player bar. Modeled on the tvOS look.

@import url('https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/safiyu/ijelly@latest/ijelly.css');

Poster-Tinted

The page takes its colors from whatever you are looking at. A blurred copy of the backdrop washes the screen, and the poster you focus glows in its own colors. CSS only, so it works on TV.

@import url('https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/safiyu/ijelly@latest/ijelly_tinted.css');

Install in three steps

  1. In Jellyfin, open Dashboard, General, then Custom CSS.
  2. Paste the import line for the theme you want.
  3. Save, then turn on Backdrops under Settings, Display.

The @latest tag follows the newest release, but jsDelivr caches it. To stay on one version, replace @latest with a release tag such as @2.0.13.

On the TV too

  • Pages use the same layout as desktop: backdrop banner, poster, buttons, then the description.
  • Home rows and library grids fit the screen with safe margins.
  • The player puts the seek bar on its own row, with the controls packed together underneath.

Tested on the Samsung Tizen app. Keep the Jellyfin app on your TV up to date.
